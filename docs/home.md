## 3. Problem

**Section label (small caps):** The Real Risk

**Villain headline:**
Generic background music doesn't just disappoint — it lets the moment slip away forever.

**Body copy:**

You've spent months on this. The venue, the catering, the flowers, the seating chart — every detail carefully chosen. But there's something about the music that still feels uncertain.

Most live music at events is forgettable. It fills silence, then fades from memory. Guests chat over it, ignore it, and by the time they leave, they couldn't tell you what was playing. The atmosphere never quite lifts. The energy stays flat.

And the worst part? You'll always wonder. *What if the music had actually been right for this?*

There's also the doubt you won't say out loud — the nagging feeling that you might be making the wrong call. That you've put so much into this event and the music is the one thing you still can't be sure about.

A momentous occasion deserves more than background noise.
