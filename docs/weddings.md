## FAQ

**Section headline:** Questions couples usually ask

**Q1: Can you learn a specific song for our ceremony or first dance?**
A: In many cases, yes. If you have a particular piece or song in mind — even a pop song you want arranged for cello — get in touch and we can discuss whether it's feasible. I've arranged many pieces specifically for clients' ceremonies.

**Q2: Do you need a PA system or amplification?**
A: For most indoor venues, no. A cello carries naturally in most ceremony and reception spaces. For larger outdoor venues or rooms with difficult acoustics, I'll advise you in advance and can arrange amplification if needed. This is something we'd discuss on our planning call.

**Q3: How much of the ceremony do you cover?**
A: I typically play through: guests arriving and being seated (prelude), the bridal procession, signing of the register, and the recessional. The package you choose determines whether I also cover the cocktail hour and/or reception. See the Rates page for full details.

**Q4: What happens if something goes wrong on the day?**
A: In over 1,200 hours of performance, I've learnt to handle the unexpected without it affecting the guests' experience. Timing adjustments, extended preludes, pauses — I read the room and adapt. You won't need to worry about a thing.

**Q5: How far in advance should we book?**
A: Cape Town wedding season (October–March) fills up fast, especially weekends. I'd recommend reaching out at least three to six months in advance if your date is in peak season. That said, if you have a shorter lead time, it's always worth getting in touch — I may still be available.

**Q6: Do you travel outside Cape Town?**
A: Yes. I regularly perform at Winelands venues — Franschhoek, Stellenbosch, Paarl — and can travel further for the right event. Travel costs would be discussed transparently on your planning call.
