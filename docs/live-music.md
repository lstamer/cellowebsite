## What I Offer

**Section label:** Performance Formats

**Headline:** Flexible formats for venues and events of every size

**Solo Cello — Background**
The most versatile format. Rich, textured cello playing that lifts the atmosphere of any space without demanding an audience's full attention. Ideal for wine bars, restaurant evenings, gallery openings, and boutique shopping events.

**Solo Cello — Featured Performance**
A dedicated set where cello takes centre stage. Works for festival stages, opening nights, special event evenings, or any context where live music is the draw, not just the backdrop. Typically 30–60 minutes.

**Venue Residency**
A regular booking — weekly or monthly — where live cello becomes part of your venue's identity. I've held residencies in Cape Town and understand how to make live music a consistent, manageable part of your programming.

**Custom Format**
