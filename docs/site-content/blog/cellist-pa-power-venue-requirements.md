## Body

Venue logistics are rarely glamorous, but they quietly decide whether live music feels effortless or stressful.

Most sound, setup, and timing issues are simple to prevent—as long as someone asks the practical questions early. If no one does, the musician ends up solving them under pressure on the day.

Below are the venue details that matter most, and why they matter.

## Power

If amplification is required, the first question is straightforward: where is the nearest reliable power source?

This matters because:

- Long cable runs and improvised extensions are safety risks and stress-inducing.
- Last-minute searches for plugs delay the start of the music.
- If the performance area is far from power, it’s far better to know in advance and plan around it (battery-powered options, alternative placement, or additional cabling).
