## Body

Outdoor weddings in Cape Town’s vineyards and estates can be extraordinary, but they ask more of your music setup than an indoor ceremony. The space, weather, and terrain all shape how live cello will actually sound and feel on the day.

## Wind is not a small detail

Rain is the obvious concern, but wind is often the bigger threat to live music.

Strong or inconsistent wind can:

- blow sheet music around
- push microphones and stands off balance
