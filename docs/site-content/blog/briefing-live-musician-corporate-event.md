## Body

A good musician can only support the event as well as they have been briefed.

When a corporate function feels polished, it is usually because the details were clear long before guests arrived. When it feels awkward, the weak point is often not the performance itself. It is the brief.

If you are booking live music for a branded event, launch, awards evening, or high-stakes client function, here is what your musician should know in advance.
