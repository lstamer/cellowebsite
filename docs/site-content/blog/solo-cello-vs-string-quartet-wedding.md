## Body

When you start planning live music for an event, it is easy to assume that bigger automatically means better: more players, more sound, more visual impact.

Sometimes that is true. Often it is not.

Choosing between solo cello and an ensemble is really about fit: the size of the space, the atmosphere you want, the role the music needs to play, and the budget you are working with.

## When solo cello is the stronger choice

Solo cello works especially well when you want elegance, warmth, and intimacy.
