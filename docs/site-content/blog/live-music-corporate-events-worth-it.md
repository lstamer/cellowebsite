## Body

Not every corporate event needs live music. That is the honest starting point.

If the event is a tightly packed internal meeting with no social component, a short budget, and no moment where atmosphere matters, live music may not add much. In those cases, recorded background sound or no music at all may be the smarter choice.

But there are also corporate events where live music changes how the room feels immediately. The difference is not whether the music is impressive in theory. The difference is whether the event benefits from atmosphere, pacing, and presence.

## When live music is usually worth it

Live music tends to justify itself best when the event has a clear hospitality or brand experience element.
