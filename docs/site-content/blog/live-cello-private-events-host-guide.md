## Body

### Private events and solo cello: key points for hosts

Private events reward a different kind of musical thinking than large weddings or public celebrations. In small rooms, music works on proximity rather than spectacle: guests are closer, the space is more personal, and tone matters more than scale.

Solo cello can be an ideal choice here. It can make a room feel considered and elevated without making it feel staged or formal to the point of stiffness. But small events are also where hosts most often misjudge how to use live music.

## Common planning mistakes

### Mistake 1: Treating a small event like a mini wedding

Intimate dinners, birthdays, anniversaries, home concerts, and private celebrations usually do **not** need wall‑to‑wall music in the way a large wedding drinks reception might.

- The room is smaller.
- Guest flow is looser and more informal.
