## What I Offer

**Section label:** The Music

**Headline:** Gentle, considered, and entirely tailored to your loved one.

**Body:**

There's no standard set list for this. Every person is different. Every farewell should reflect the life that was lived.

I'll work with you to choose music that feels right — whether that's a traditional hymn, a classical piece they loved, a contemporary song that meant something to them, or simply something peaceful that gives the room space to breathe.
