## Format Options

**Section label:** What Works for Your Event

**Headline:** Flexible formats for every type of corporate function

**Option 1 — Cocktail & Networking Set**
Background performance during the welcome reception or networking period. Sophisticated ambience while guests arrive and mingle. Typically 45–60 minutes.

**Option 2 — Dining Background**
Continuous background cello through dinner service. Elevates the meal without interrupting conversation. Typically 60–90 minutes.

**Option 3 — Featured Performance**
A dedicated 20–30 minute set where guests are briefly invited to listen — ideal as a transition moment between dinner and speeches, or before the entertainment programme begins.

**Option 4 — Full Evening**
A combination of the above across a full event timeline. We'll design the structure together based on your programme.

## FAQ

**Section headline:** What event managers usually ask

**Q1: Can you play background music through dinner and also do a featured set?**
A: Yes. Many corporate events use both — background cello through the cocktail and dining phases, and a more deliberate featured set as a transition moment during the programme. We'd design this together based on your run-of-show.

**Q2: What are your setup and space requirements?**
A: I need a small area (approximately 1.5m × 1.5m), a chair, and ideally a few minutes to warm up before guests arrive. For larger venues or events with amplification already in place, I can connect to your PA system. I'll confirm all requirements on our planning call.

**Q3: Do you need amplification?**
A: For most indoor corporate venues — dining rooms, hotel ballrooms, boardroom settings — a cello projects naturally without PA support. For very large spaces or outdoor functions, amplification is available. We'll identify what your venue needs in advance.

**Q4: How do you handle last-minute changes to the programme?**
A: I'm used to live events where timings shift. If speeches run over or dinner is delayed, I'll extend or adjust the set accordingly. You have one less thing to worry about.

**Q5: Can you provide music for a product launch or brand event?**
A: Yes. Live cello works well for brand activations, product reveals, and marketing events where you want a premium, distinctive atmosphere. If you have something specific in mind, let's talk.
